## Setup (fill in before running)

**App name:** Ab Bekhoor
**Category:** Health & Fitness
**ICP:** Active adults, 25–45, who want to hit a daily hydration goal without
an app that gamifies, nags, or requires accounts. Currently using Notes or a

no gamification. Log, check progress, done.
**Key features:**
1. Log 250ml, 500ml, or custom in one tap
2. Daily goal based on weight and activity (editable)
3. Streak tracking (opt-in — shown but not emphasized)

## Generate the following

### 1. App Name options (3 variations)
30 characters max. The name "Ab Bekhoor" is already set — generate subtitle
variations only if the name is locked.

### 2. Subtitle (3 variations)
30 characters max. Should contain the primary search keyword naturally.
Complements the name; don't restate it.

### 3. Promotional text (2 variations)
170 characters max. This updates without a new app version — write for
a specific moment (e.g., summer, New Year) or a current promotion.
Not for evergreen copy.

### 4. Description (1 draft)
4000 characters max. Structure:
- Lines 1–3: shown before "more" fold — lead with the differentiator,
  not the feature list. This is where most readers stop.
- Feature highlights: 3–4 short bullets
- Use case paragraph: one scenario, specific, for the ICP
- Close: one line

Constraints:
- No "Download now" opener
- No superlatives ("best," "most powerful," "ultimate")
- No generic claims ("easy to use," "beautiful design," "intuitive")
- Assume the reader already wants a water tracker — your job is to
  convince them this one is different
- Do not mention competitors by name

### 5. Keywords (1 list)
100 characters max including commas.
- Do not repeat words already in the App Name or Subtitle
- Focus on: search terms someone would use who wants exactly what this
  app does — not who wants "a water app" generically
- Include Persian-language terms if relevant: آب, نوشیدن آب

### 6. Brief notes on what you left out
What claims couldn't you make with the information given?
What would strengthen the listing if you had it (e.g., user count,
ratings, a specific customer quote)?
